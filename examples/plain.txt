Converter Operating Notes

doc2toon profiles a document before converting it. This matters because TOON is useful when repeated structure can be represented once and streamed as rows.

The goal is useful context density, not maximum prose fidelity. Redundant phrasing, repeated ideas, and decorative padding should not survive merely because they were present in the source.

Lossless prose may not shrink. If the target budget is smaller than the retained semantic text, budget mode must either refuse or clearly mark semantic compression as lossy.
